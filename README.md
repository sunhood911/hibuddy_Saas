Hibuddy Bot (@hibuddy_ai_bot) is a **Telegram access control bot** that turns any Telegram group into a **paid membership group**. Group owners monetize their communities with zero setup cost: no monthly subscription, no hidden fees — pay only a **flat 5% service fee when an order is completed**.

Looking for an **InviteMember alternative**, **Whop alternative**, **LaunchPass alternative**, or a **Patreon / Substack style subscription bot for Telegram**? Hibuddy is the lightweight, no-subscription way to sell access to your Telegram group.

- **Automated gatekeeper** — new members join only after payment; non-payers are rejected automatically

- **Payments go directly to the group owner** — the platform never holds your funds
- **Multi-language** — English, 繁體中文, Русский, Español
- **Full automation** — group owner only sets price and duration once; the bot handles the rest 24/7
